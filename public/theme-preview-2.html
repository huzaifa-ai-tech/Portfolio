What the top sites are doing right now

  • Text-first work indexes — winners like huyml.co (Huy Phan) list projects as giant typographic rows; hovering reveals the image. Simple, confident, very "designer".
  • Developer portfolios win with personality — nahiyan.codes (SOTD + Developer Award) is almost all text: "today", "yesterday", a personal voice, small playful details. No heavy effects.
  • Monochrome + ONE loud color — off-white/charcoal base with a single vivid accent (orange, green, amber, blue). Never many colors at once.
  • Personality details — local time in the corner, availability badge, sound toggle, "playground" section, serif italic accent words.
  • Cinematic but controlled motion — staggered reveals, marquees, hover image reveals, sticky scroll scenes. One strong idea per page, not ten small ones.
